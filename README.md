## Features
- **Theme:** Royal Krishna (Royal Blue `#0c1c40`, Antique Gold `#d4af37`, Ivory)
- **Opening Experience:** Immersive Video Intro with Radha Krishna Divine Blessing & Tap to Open
- **Background Music:** *Kalyanam Vybhogam* from *Srinivasa Kalyanam* with floating toggle
- **Interactive Countdown:** Scratch-to-reveal golden foil countdown timer
- **Photo Gallery:** Centered, touch-swipe carousel with navigation dots and arrows
- **Stacking Events:** GSAP ScrollTrigger 3D card deck stacking
- **Venues:** Dual embedded Google Maps with one-click directions
- **RSVP:** Interactive RSVP form with guest details auto-toggle & Google Sheets integration
- **Sharing:** Pre-filled WhatsApp invitation card and link copy with toast notifications
- **Metadata:** OpenGraph / WhatsApp preview banner (1200x630) with portraits and gold borders
